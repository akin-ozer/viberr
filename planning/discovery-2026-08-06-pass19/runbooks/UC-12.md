# UC-12 — Operator recommends impl→review; human applies the recommendation (R15-3)

## Purpose

Proves the supervised-operator recommendation loop across the `approval` boundary, and
**ruling 22 / R15-3** (owner authority covers stage-transition recommendations — the Apply
click IS the authorization for that one move):

1. `operatorTransitionStage` (`app/server/tasks/operator-actions.server.ts:1906-1953`):
   with `stage-transitions: recommend` and a non-`auto`, non-rework boundary, the operator
   posts a `transition` **recommendation card** ("Move the task to <Review>") instead of
   moving the task. (Auto boundaries are crossed directly even under `recommend` — that
   asymmetry is deliberate and is what lets triage→ready→impl self-drive.)
2. `applyRecommendation` (`app/server/tasks/task-actions.server.ts:5411-5585`), the
   `transition` branch (:5506-5533): maintainer+ **OR the task's own owner** may apply; an
   owner below the `approve-transition` tier gets `recommendationAuthorized: true` —
   relaxed ONLY on this path, never on a bare stage-menu move. Declared edges keep their
   boundary semantics.
3. The applied card is removed by id, audited `task.recommendation.applied`, and the
   transition re-invokes the operator at the new stage (`autoInvokeOperator`, trigger
   `transition`).

Known context: **F19-1 (filed) / R19-4 (ruled)** — under supervised autonomy nothing
server-side yet *guarantees* the post-delivery recommendation exists; whether the operator
records it is model-dependent (VC-4/VC-5 did, VC-1 did not). This runbook's arrangement
includes the nudge fallback and records F19-1 evidence when it reproduces.

  boundary → **NEW HIGH** (`operatorTransitionStage` gate broken; only `auto` boundaries
  and R7-4 rework moves may bypass the recommend gate).
- No transition recommendation after delivery + nudge → **F19-1** evidence (filed;
  R19-4 pending) — record run ids and the operator's narration verbatim.
- Elif-as-owner Apply → 403 → **NEW HIGH**: R15-3 regression (the F15-12 dead-end
  returns: a card shown with an Apply the server then refuses).
- Elif-non-owner Apply → 200 → **NEW HIGH**: decision authority widened beyond
  R14-2/R15-3.
- Double-apply produces two transitions or the 409 comes with a second transition event →
  **NEW MED** idempotency (NFR16).
- Card remains in `recommendations` after a 200 apply → **NEW MED** (cleared-by-id write
  failed; stale "Waiting on you" would follow).
- Applying does not clear the approval notification (bell keeps the row) → **NEW LOW**
  (`markTaskPacketApprovalRead` path).
- Operator's card label/reasoning uses "primary specialist" → **F19-12** (filed).
